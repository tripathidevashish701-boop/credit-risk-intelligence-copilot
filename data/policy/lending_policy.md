# Fictional Lending Policy (Demo Only)

## Identity and income verification
Applicants should provide valid identity documentation and evidence of current address.
Income verification may include recent salary slips, bank statements, or tax documents.
Self-employed applicants may provide audited financial statements or tax returns.
Documents must be current, legible, and consistent with the application.

## Repayment capacity
Analysts should assess existing monthly obligations alongside verified income.
A high existing EMI-to-income ratio can indicate limited repayment capacity.
Loan amount and tenure should be evaluated together rather than in isolation.
A policy exception requires documented rationale and an authorized human review.

## Credit bureau review
Review bureau score, repayment history, recent delinquencies, and active obligations.
A thin credit file should not automatically be treated as evidence of poor repayment behaviour.
Where bureau data is unavailable or inconsistent, request clarification and follow the institution's approved alternate assessment process.

## Delinquency indicators
Recent past-due events may warrant additional verification and closer review.
A single indicator should not determine an outcome; consider the complete verified profile.
All final decisions require human review and applicable legal and fair-lending checks.

## Data quality and governance
Use only data collected for an authorized purpose.
Record data sources, transformations, model version, and reviewer rationale.
Escalate missing, conflicting, or potentially discriminatory data for review.
